Welcome to Jallikattu

Jallikattu is a traditional bull-taming sport celebrated in parts of Tamil Nadu, especially during the Pongal season. It is closely connected with village life, farming traditions and the relationship between farmers and native cattle.

The event is an important part of Tamil cultural identity. It brings communities together and highlights the role of cattle in agriculture and rural life.

What this website covers

  • The history and cultural background of Jallikattu.
  • Its connection with Pongal and farmers.
  • Native cattle breeds and the importance of animal care.

Important: Events should be conducted only under applicable rules, with proper safety arrangements and care for people and animals.
