About Jallikattu

History and Meaning

Jallikattu is a long-standing rural tradition of Tamil Nadu. It is commonly associated with the harvest season and Mattu Pongal celebrations, when cattle are honoured for their contribution to farming.

References to bull-related traditions are found in Tamil cultural history. Over time, Jallikattu became a well-known symbol of village heritage and community celebration.

Connection with Farmers

Cattle have traditionally helped farming communities with agricultural work and have held an important place in rural life. Jallikattu also draws attention to native cattle breeds and cattle-rearing families.

Today, the tradition is discussed in the context of culture, animal welfare and public safety. Respect for animals and compliance with the law are essential.

Native Cattle Breeds

  • Kangayam
  • Pulikulam
  • Umbalachery
