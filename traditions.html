Traditions & Culture

Jallikattu takes place during the Pongal season, a harvest celebration that gives thanks for the harvest, the Sun, farmers and cattle. Mattu Pongal is the day especially dedicated to cattle.

Celebrating Pongal

Pongal and Community

Pongal is a major harvest festival in Tamil Nadu. Families prepare traditional food, decorate homes and celebrate the importance of farming. Jallikattu is one of the rural events associated with this season.

Culture and Responsibility

Traditional celebrations can be preserved while giving importance to animal welfare, medical support, crowd management and legal requirements. Responsible planning helps protect everyone involved.
